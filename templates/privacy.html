Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you give us: your account details such as name and email address, and the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic technical information needed to run the service, such as session data that keeps you signed in.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide the app's features, including logging expenses, showing category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and filtering by date range. We use account details to sign you in and to contact you about your account. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy. We do not share your expense records with advertisers or other third parties, except where required by law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details published on the website.
